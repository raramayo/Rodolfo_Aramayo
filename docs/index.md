## From a biological question to the next test

<figure class="ra-research-flow">
  <ol aria-label="Four connected stages of the laboratory's research approach">
    <li><span class="ra-flow-number" aria-hidden="true">01</span><h3>Frame the question</h3><p>Use biological mechanism and the literature to define a testable problem.</p></li>
    <li><span class="ra-flow-number" aria-hidden="true">02</span><h3>Establish the evidence</h3><p>Examine experimental design, sequence quality, annotations, and reference data.</p></li>
    <li><span class="ra-flow-number" aria-hidden="true">03</span><h3>Build and challenge</h3><p>Run reproducible analyses, compare baselines, and investigate failure cases.</p></li>
    <li><span class="ra-flow-number" aria-hidden="true">04</span><h3>Define the next test</h3><p>Separate supported conclusions from hypotheses that need additional evidence.</p></li>
  </ol>
  <figcaption>Our research approach connects experimental genetics, computational analysis, and biological interpretation.</figcaption>
</figure>
