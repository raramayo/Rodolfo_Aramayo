# Funding & Research Support

Research depends on different kinds of support. Computing allocations, sponsored awards, and controlled-data access serve distinct purposes; they are not interchangeable measures of grant funding.

## Computing for research and education

Digital Biology has received national computing allocations through XSEDE and ACCESS under BIO210035, with entries spanning 2021–2026. These resources support hands-on computational training and the use of shared scientific infrastructure.

## Sponsored research and institutional awards

Selected support spans molecular genetics, genomics, interdisciplinary software, and undergraduate research:

- **Molecular genetics and cell biology:** NIH-supported studies of meiotic trans-sensing and silencing, and coupling protein synthesis with cell division.
- **Reproducible scientific infrastructure:** Materials Galaxy workflow-management projects and institutional Big Data seed support.
- **Research and education:** the Aggie Undergraduate Genomics Corps, interdisciplinary collaborations, and Texas A&M seed awards.

## Authorized research-data access

NIH/NCBI dbGaP authorizations supported investigations of structural variation and gene structure. These entries document controlled-data access, **not monetary awards**.
